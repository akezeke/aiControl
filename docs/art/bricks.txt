Brick Knights artwork. Raster images were generated with Codex's built-in imagegen tool (codex exec, one image per run) and saved as WebP;
the brick pieces the interface is built from are live geometry (client/bricks3d), so they sit on one exact grid.

Assets (client/public unless noted), all sized by scripts/bricks-art.py from docs/art/src:
bricks-bg.webp               bg2 prompt below, then edited once (bg4) to add a stone pier for the drawbridge to dock on
bricks-theme.webp            emblem2
providers/bricks-{codex,claude,opencode}.webp   icons2 (2 x 2 atlas, three used)
bricks-portraits.webp        portraits2 (2 x 2 atlas)
bricks-dragon.webp           dragon2
spend-bricks.webp            chest
bricks-fig-{ranger,herald,knight,dragon}.webp   figs (cut apart)
bricks-torch.webp, bricks-banner.webp           torch, banner
bricks-gate-{left,right}.webp                   gate2, split down the seam

The bricks the interface is built from are not images. client/bricks3d builds them live in three.js (bevelled boxes with lathe-turned studs, real proportions:
stud pitch 8 mm, brick 9.6 mm, plate 3.2 mm, drawn at 2.5 px per mm), and paints each element's text, icons and portraits onto the brick face as a texture.
"LEGO" is a trademark of the LEGO Group, which does not sponsor or endorse this project; the theme is an original design in the style of toy castle bricks.

[bg4, an edit of the bg2 result]
Use case: precise-object-edit
Edit the attached image (ref.png). Keep EVERYTHING exactly as it is - same castle, sky, clouds, mist, mountains, trees, lighting, colours, framing, resolution and photographic style - except for one change at the river in the lower right:

The lowered wooden drawbridge in front of the castle gate ends in the middle of the river and touches nothing. Do NOT lengthen or extend the wooden drawbridge and do NOT add any wooden planks anywhere. Keep the drawbridge exactly the length it is now.

Instead, build a solid STONE landing pier made of weathered grey plastic building bricks (dark and light bluish-grey bricks, visible studs on top, stacked brick courses, a crenellated or flat stone top) that rises out of the water on the far (left-hand) side of the drawbridge's free end. It starts at the left riverbank and juts out a good way into the river as a stone quay / pier with its sides standing in the water, ending right where the drawbridge's free end comes down, so that the end of the drawbridge rests ON the stone pier top, at the same height, with a visible clean seat. The pier is the thing the drawbridge docks to, like the fixed stone abutment of a drawbridge crossing; the water continues to flow around its front and sides. Same lighting, warm torch glow, reflections in the water, and shallow depth of field as the rest of the image, so it looks like it was always part of the diorama.

Do not change anything else. No text, no logos, no watermark.
